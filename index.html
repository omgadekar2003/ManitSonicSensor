The Problem

Large vehicles like trucks and buses have massive blind spots. Drivers cannot see pedestrians, cyclists, or vehicles nearby, leading to fatal accidents during turns and reversing.

💡

Our Solution

Manit's Sonic Sensor uses ultrasonic sensors, AI camera, and real-time alerts to detect danger. Instant visual and audio warnings help drivers react immediately.

🏆

Unique USP

Low cost, easy installation on ANY old vehicle. AI + sensor fusion technology at affordable price. Retrofit existing fleet without expensive upgrades.

Key Features

📏

Distance Measurement

Ultrasonic sensors measure up to 4 meters with 1cm accuracy. Real-time distance tracking for multiple zones.

🤖

AI Object Detection

Camera identifies humans, vehicles, animals. Stepper motor rotates camera toward threat direction automatically.

🔔

Smart Alert System

Green LED = Safe | Yellow LED = Caution | Red LED + Buzzer + Vibration = Danger — Multi-stage warnings.

📱

Future Ready

Mobile app integration, voice alerts, automatic braking system — upgradeable design for future enhancements.
