The Problem

Cargo trucks have massive blind spots, especially on the right side and rear. Drivers cannot see smaller vehicles, cyclists, or pedestrians, leading to fatal accidents during turns and lane changes.

💡

Our Solution

Manit's Sonic Sensor uses ultrasonic sensors, AI camera, and real-time alerts to detect danger around cargo trucks. Instant warnings help drivers react immediately.

🏆

Unique USP

Low cost, easy installation on ANY cargo truck. AI + sensor fusion technology at affordable price. Retrofit existing fleet without expensive upgrades.

Key Features

📏

Distance Measurement

Ultrasonic sensors measure up to 4 meters with 1cm accuracy. Real-time distance tracking for multiple zones around the truck.

🤖

AI Object Detection

Camera identifies humans, vehicles, animals. Stepper motor rotates camera toward threat direction automatically.

🔔

Smart Alert System

Green = Safe | Yellow = Caution | Red + Buzzer + Vibration = Danger — Multi-stage warnings for drivers.

📱

Future Ready

Mobile app integration, voice alerts, automatic braking system — upgradeable design for future enhancements.
